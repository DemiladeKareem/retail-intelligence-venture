# Retail Intelligence Venture

A retail intelligence project built around a synthetic Nigerian multi-branch retailer to demonstrate how sales, inventory, margin, product, customer and branch data can be transformed into actionable management insights.

## Project Overview

The project simulates a realistic Nigerian retail operation with multiple branches, products, suppliers, customers and operational activities.

The objective is to build an end-to-end retail analytics solution that moves from raw business data to validated metrics, analytical insights and management reporting.

## Business Questions

The analysis is designed to answer questions such as:

- What is actually selling?
- Which products and categories drive revenue and profit?
- What inventory is tying up cash?
- Which products are at risk of stocking out?
- Where are margins changing?
- How effective are discounts and promotions?
- Which branches are performing strongly or weakly?
- What factors are driving changes in business performance?

## Synthetic Retail Environment

The project will include synthetic data covering:

- Branches
- Products and categories
- Suppliers
- Customers
- Sales transactions
- Purchases
- Inventory snapshots
- Discounts and promotions

The data will also contain controlled real-world challenges such as:

- Stockouts
- Slow-moving and dead stock
- Margin compression
- Promotional activity
- Seasonal demand
- Product concentration
- Data-quality issues

- Python

## Project Workflow

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

1. Business and analytical requirements
2. Data architecture and modelling
3. Synthetic data generation
4. Data cleaning and validation
5. SQL staging and transformation
6. Exploratory and business analysis
7. KPI development
8. Power BI reporting
9. Management insights and recommended actions

## Analytical Areas

The solution focuses on:

- Sales performance
- Revenue growth
- Gross profit and gross margin
- Product and category performance
- Inventory value and movement
- Inventory turnover
- Slow-moving and dead stock
- Stockout risk
- Supplier and purchasing activity
- Branch performance
- Customer behaviour
- Promotion effectiveness

## Retail Business Pulse

The final analytical output will be structured around a management reporting solution called **Retail Business Pulse**.

The reporting experience will cover:

1. Owner Overview
2. Sales & Margin Intelligence
3. Product & Inventory Intelligence
4. Branch & Customer Intelligence
5. Action Centre

The objective is not simply to display KPIs, but to connect business performance to the issues and opportunities that require management attention.
